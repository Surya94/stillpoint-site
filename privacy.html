Privacy Policy

Applies to: Balloon Archer (Android, package com.stillpoint.balloonarcher)
Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Balloon Archer, published by Still Point Games ("we", "us"). We are based in India.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through AppLovin MAX. AppLovin and its advertising partners may collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, to show and measure ads.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your phone. We cannot access it, and it is not sent to us:

  • Your best score
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings
  • Whether you have already seen the first-shot tutorial hint

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes this information.

3. Advertising

Balloon Archer is free and supported by ads, including optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to continue a run) and full-screen ads between games. Ads are provided by AppLovin MAX and the advertising networks it works with ("mediation partners").

To serve, personalise (where you allow it), limit how often you see, and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, these companies may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and operating system information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • App information and ad interactions (ads shown, clicked or watched)

This collection is done by those companies under their own privacy policies. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. See AppLovin's Privacy Policy, which also lists its partners.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar rules, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's settings, where available, or by clearing the app's data so the prompt appears again.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). With no ID, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

4. Children

Balloon Archer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a user is a child, we take steps so that only non-personalised ads are shown to them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

5. How long information is kept

Data on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. How long advertising partners keep data is set out in their own privacy policies.

6. Security

The game does not send your progress or settings to any server. Ad traffic is sent over encrypted connections by the ad SDK.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. These include:

  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): the right to information about processing, to correction and erasure, to withdraw consent, to nominate someone, and to grievance redressal.
  • EEA / UK (GDPR): the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, the right to withdraw consent, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar): the right to know, delete and correct, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID as described above.

Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests about advertising data are best sent to AppLovin. We will still help with any request; contact us below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features or advertising partners. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be noted on this page.
